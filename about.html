About Nova

A workforce partner, not a phone number.

Nova Construction Solutions SA helps projects across South Australia get the right people, tickets and support — from a single labourer for a day through to workforce planning across a program.

What Nova does

Two needs, one point of contact.

Nova Construction Solutions Pty Ltd supplies construction labour hire, skilled trades and project support to builders, contractors and project teams. The business exists to solve a narrow problem well: construction projects need the right people on site at the right time, and finding them shouldn't slow a program down.

We work across two related needs — supplying workforce to businesses that need labour and trades, and providing direct site support to project teams that need more than staffing. Both run through the same person, so nothing gets lost between departments.

How we work

Right person, right job

Workers are matched against what the scope actually needs. Sending a warm body to fill a line on a roster helps nobody.

Direct communication

Site supervisors deal with Nova directly. No account layers, no relay, no waiting for someone to call someone else.

Reliability over volume

A smaller number of well-matched placements is worth more than a big number that turns over every week.

Safety & quality

Safety is set by the site and upheld by the worker.

Every site has its own safety requirements, induction and supervision structure. Nova's role is making sure workers arrive ready to follow them — briefed on expectations before deployment, with current tickets, and clear that site procedures take precedence from the moment they walk through the gate.

Work health and safety obligations vary by project and jurisdiction and remain subject to applicable law. Nothing on this site changes the responsibilities of the parties controlling a worksite.

Quality means the right match.

A placement is only successful if the worker fits the requirement. Nova measures itself on accurate matching — trade, experience, tickets, availability — rather than on how quickly a gap gets closed.

If someone isn't the right fit for your site, tell us. We'd rather fix it the same day than have you quietly stop calling.
