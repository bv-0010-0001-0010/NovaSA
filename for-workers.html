For workers

Got a trade and a ticket? Let's talk.

Nova places labourers, trades and plant operators on sites across South Australia. Register your details and we'll consider you for jobs as they come up.

Register with Nova

What we look for

Current tickets

A valid Construction Induction Card (White Card) is the minimum. Extra tickets — heights, EWP, confined space, forklift, traffic control — open up more work.

You turn up

On time, in the right PPE, ready to work. It sounds obvious. It's the single thing site supervisors ask us about most.

Straight answers

Tell us what you've actually done and what you're actually available for. We'd rather place you well once than badly three times.

Registering

Four things to have ready.

Your trade

What you do, and how long you've been doing it.

Your tickets

White Card number plus anything else current — we'll need to sight them before a placement.

Your availability

Days, weeks, ongoing. Whether you can travel, and how far.

Right to work

Australian citizenship, permanent residency or a visa with valid work rights.

Tickets that open doors

  • Construction Induction Card (White Card)
  • Working at Heights
  • Confined Space
  • EWP (Elevated Work Platform)
  • Forklift licence (LF)
  • Traffic Management / TCT
  • Asbestos awareness
  • First Aid

Don't have all of them? Register anyway. General labouring work starts with a White Card, and plenty of people build the rest up from there.

Register with Nova

Registering isn't a job offer.

It puts you on our list for work that matches your trade, tickets and availability. If something fits, we'll email you if a suitable opportunity arises.
