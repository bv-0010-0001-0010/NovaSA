Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains how Nova Construction Solutions SA Pty Ltd handles personal information.

Who we are

Nova Construction Solutions SA Pty Ltd of 61 Stony Brook Way, Truganina VIC 3029 (“Nova”, “we”, “us”) is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to enquiries and provide our services. Depending on your dealings with us, that may include:

  • Contact details — name, company, email address, phone number and site location.
  • For workers — trade, work history, licences and tickets, availability, and right-to-work information you choose to provide.
  • Enquiry content you choose to send us through this website, by email or by phone.
  • Basic technical information such as pages visited, collected in aggregate.

How we collect it

Directly from you — through the enquiry form on this website, by email, by phone or in person. Enquiries submitted through this website are transmitted to a Google Workspace account controlled by Nova.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and supply the services you have asked about.
  • To match workers to roles and to verify tickets, licences and right to work.
  • To administer services and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we disclose it to

We may disclose personal information to a host client or principal contractor where necessary to arrange or manage a placement, to our professional advisers and service providers (including payroll, accounting and IT providers), and to a government body or regulator where required or authorised by law.

Storage and security

Information is held in cloud-based systems that may store data in Australia or overseas. We take reasonable steps to protect information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Access, correction and complaints

You may ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing admin@novaconstructions.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied with our response to a privacy complaint, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this privacy policy page.
