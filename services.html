Services

Workforce, matched to the scope.

Four services that overlap. Builders normally start with labour hire and pull in the rest as the program tightens.

01 · Labour hire

Construction labour hire

General and skilled labourers, available by the day, the week or the length of a program.

Useful when a program moves, a subcontractor drops off, weather pushes everything right, or a stage needs more hands than the permanent crew can cover. You keep supervision and site control; Nova handles sourcing, availability and the administration behind the placement.

Typical rolesGeneral labourers, skilled labourers, trade assistants, site cleaners, demolition labourers
EngagementDay work, short-term surge, ongoing placement
Standard ticketsConstruction Induction Card (White Card) as a minimum; further tickets to scope
SupervisionYour site, your supervision, your induction process

02 · Skilled trades

Skilled trades

Trade-specific workers matched against the actual scope, with tickets checked before they are put forward.

A placement only works if the person fits the job. Nova treats accurate matching — on trade, experience, tickets and availability — as the measure of a good outcome, not how fast a gap gets filled.

Trades suppliedCarpenters, formworkers, steel fixers, concreters, bricklayers, plasterers, tilers, painters, waterproofers, roof plumbers
Plant operatorsExcavator, bobcat, EWP and forklift operators to ticket
VerificationTickets and licences sighted and recorded before placement
Lead timeShorter for common trades; specialist scopes need more notice

03 · Workforce solutions

Workforce solutions

Ongoing planning and supply for builders running several sites at once.

Rather than a fresh scramble every time a stage starts, Nova works to your forecast — what is coming, when, and how many. Numbers scale up and down against the program instead of against a phone call made the night before.

Best forBuilders and contractors with rolling stages or multiple concurrent sites
How it runsForecast against your program, then supply to the schedule
ContactOne point of contact across every site
FlexibilityScale up for a pour or a handover, scale back after

04 · Site & project support

Site & project support

The tasks that quietly hold up a program when nobody is free to do them.

Site clean-downs, materials handling, loading and unloading, waste segregation, traffic control and pre-handover preparation. Small scopes, but they sit on the critical path more often than they should.

Site presentationProgressive clean-downs, waste segregation, pre-handover clean
MaterialsLoading, unloading, distribution around site
TrafficTicketed traffic controllers for deliveries and works zones
AccessLabour support for temporary fencing, hoarding and edge protection

Trade, site, start date and how long you need it. That's enough for us to come back with what we can cover.
